Each spring, vast blooms of phytoplankton spread across the North Atlantic, supporting marine food webs and helping regulate Earth’s climate. Integrating satellite observations, ocean colour data and modelling, Copernicus Marine Service allows these seasonal blooms to be explored and monitored through its MyOcean visualisation tools.
