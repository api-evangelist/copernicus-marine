The Ocean is both a driver and a major casualty of the accelerating climate crisis, and we need to transform scientific knowledge into tangible action. This was the urgent message delivered at the Forum Météo et Climat Media Workshop, held earlier this month at the European Digital Ocean Pavilion in Nice, France.
