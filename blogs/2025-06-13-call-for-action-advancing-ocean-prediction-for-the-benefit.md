On 11 June, a global Call to Action was launched at the European Digital Ocean Pavilion, urging international mobilization to significantly enhance Ocean prediction capabilities in response to societal needs. Led by Mercator Ocean, in partnership with OceanPredict/ForeSea, GOOS, and EU4OceanObs, this initiative is aligned with UN Ocean Decade priorities.
