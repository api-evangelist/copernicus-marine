Ponta Delgada, Azores, 2-3 October 2025 - European experts and policymakers gathered at the 4th NEREUS Regions Symposium to explore how satellite and ocean data are accelerating sustainable regional development. Copernicus Marine featured prominently through various use cases, demonstrating how its data and services are helping regions unlock the full potential of the Blue Economy.
