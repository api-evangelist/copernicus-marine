The 10th Ocean State Report (OSR) from the Copernicus Marine Service is now available, marking a decade of authoritative ocean science. Published alongside its summary, and a special anniversary supplement, this year’s edition reveals how the ocean has changed over the past decade and what that means for ecosystems, communities and policymakers.
