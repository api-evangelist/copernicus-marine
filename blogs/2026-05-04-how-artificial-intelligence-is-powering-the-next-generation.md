Artificial intelligence (AI) is rapidly reshaping operational oceanography. Across the Copernicus Marine Service, a new generation of products are in development that blend physics-based modelling with advanced machine learning and deep learning techniques. The result is a structural shift in how ocean forecasts are produced, corrected, and delivered.
