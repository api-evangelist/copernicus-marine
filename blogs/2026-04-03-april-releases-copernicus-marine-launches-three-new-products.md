Copernicus Marine continues to expand its portfolio of operational ocean intelligence with the release of three new and updated products. Together, they provide new insights into Arctic change, large-scale ocean circulation, and the consistency of global sea surface temperature observations, supporting a wide range of scientific, operational, and policy applications.
